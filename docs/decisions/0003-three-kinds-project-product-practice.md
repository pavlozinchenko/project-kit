# 0003 — Three kinds: project, product, practice

- Date: 2026-10-17
- Status: accepted (amends 0002: replaces "area"); Initiatives table replaced by Next tasks in 0006

## Context
"Area" sounded odd for a site or a game, and a single ongoing kind blurred two different things: a thing people use, and an activity done for a reason.

## Decision
- **project**: has a final goal, can be archived. Finish line is "Done when".
- **product**: no final goal, but tasks and sub-projects; a site, game, app, brand. Finish line is "Healthy when".
- **practice**: has structure and a stated reason, but no concrete goal, only a direction (a vector, never reached); accounting, the yard, learning. Finish line is "Direction", optionally "Healthy when" for rhythm.
Products and practices carry an Initiatives table. In Ukrainian chat: проєкт, продукт, практика. "Рішення" was rejected because it collides with decision records (ADR).

## Alternatives considered
- "solution" / "рішення" — collides with ADR in Ukrainian.
- "system", "stream", "asset" — neutral but vaguer than product/practice.

## Consequences
Three forms of the Finish line field; registry and templates updated; the hub itself is a product.
